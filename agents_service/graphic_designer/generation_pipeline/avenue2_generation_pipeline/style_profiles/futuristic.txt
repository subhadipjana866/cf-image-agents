STYLE_NAME: Futuristic
STYLE_DESCRIPTION: Sci-fi editorial for the near-future world. Futuristic design draws
from cyberpunk aesthetics, HUD interfaces, aerospace engineering, and speculative
technology — but always in service of product storytelling. The visual language says:
this object belongs to a world that is faster, smarter, and more powerful than today.

==============================================================================
COLOR_SYSTEM
==============================================================================
PHILOSOPHY: Dark foundations with precision-lit neon accents. The colour system
references digital displays, laser optics, and deep space — high luminance against
extreme darkness creates the characteristic "Futuristic" glow.

PRIMARY PALETTE (Signature Neon):
  - Void Black:            #020208  (primary background — near-true black with blue undertone)
  - Panel Charcoal:        #0C0E18  (secondary surface — slightly lighter for depth layering)
  - Electric Cyan:         #00F5FF  (primary accent — neon glow, outlines, UI elements)
  - Plasma Violet:         #9B00FF  (secondary accent — energy fields, gradient elements)
  - Chrome White:          #E8EEFF  (primary typography on dark — cool-tinted white)

EXTENDED PALETTE (select ONE per composition):
  - Laser Red:             #FF2D55  (danger, speed, limited edition energy)
  - Acid Lime:             #B0FF00  (tech-forward, sustainability, bio-tech)
  - Solar Orange:          #FF8C00  (warmth-tech hybrid, accessible futurism)
  - Ice Blue:              #80D8FF  (clean tech, medical, aerospace)

GRADIENT LANGUAGE:
  - "Dark Matter" gradient: Void Black (#020208) → Panel Charcoal (#0C0E18) — for backgrounds
  - "Energy Pulse" gradient: Electric Cyan (#00F5FF) → Plasma Violet (#9B00FF) — for accents
  - "Neon Edge" glow: a soft 15–25px outer glow in the primary neon colour applied
    to edges of key UI elements, product outlines, or typographic anchors

TYPOGRAPHY COLOURS:
  - Primary headline: Chrome White (#E8EEFF) for maximum readability on dark
  - Accent label: Electric Cyan (#00F5FF) with optional subtle glow
  - Secondary text: #6A7A9B (muted blue-grey for hierarchy and readability)
  - Price / date callout: gradient from Electric Cyan to Plasma Violet applied horizontally

FORBIDDEN COLOUR BEHAVIOURS:
  - No warm colour temperatures (no yellows, oranges, reds unless as a designated accent)
  - No desaturated or earth-toned palettes
  - No light backgrounds (no white, cream, beige, light grey)
  - No pastel tints
  - No more than 2 neon accent colours per composition

==============================================================================
MATERIAL_VOCABULARY
==============================================================================
SURFACES:
  - Anodised matte black metal — primary product surface treatment for tech products
  - Polished chrome / mirror-finish metal — for product accents, reflective panels
  - Dark carbon fibre weave — structural texture for premium tech/automotive-adjacent products
  - Holographic foil surface — subtle rainbow iridescence for labels or packaging
  - Cracked tempered glass / fractured acrylic — for energy-burst or impact visual effects
  - Liquid metal / mercury pool — for conceptual or lifestyle products

BACKGROUND ELEMENTS:
  - Grid overlay: fine 1px lines in Electric Cyan at 8–12% opacity forming a perspective grid
  - Particle fields: scattered fine dot clusters (1–2px) in the primary neon colour at low opacity
  - Circuit trace lines: thin branching lines suggesting PCB trace routes
  - Data streams: vertical or diagonal bands of tiny text characters (matrix-style) at 5% opacity
  - Volumetric light cone: single beam of light entering frame at 45° from upper corner

PRODUCT TREATMENT:
  - Products are rendered against dark backgrounds with edge-lighting in the primary neon colour
  - Specular highlights are sharp and precise (unlike Luxury's soft diffusion)
  - Products may have holographic label elements or UI-style overlay graphics
  - Floating mid-air is strongly preferred — no surface contact or grounding

==============================================================================
LIGHTING_ENVIRONMENT
==============================================================================
PRIMARY LIGHT SOURCE: Edge-lighting from below or behind the product in Electric Cyan (#00F5FF)
  — creates the characteristic "backlit" or "rim-lit" sci-fi appearance.
SECONDARY LIGHT: Subtle overhead key light in Chrome White to reveal product form.
ACCENT LIGHT: Plasma Violet (#9B00FF) fill from the opposite side of the cyan rim —
  creates the dual-neon chromatic aberration effect.
SHADOW STYLE: Hard, crisp shadows cast in dark blue-black on any surface contact.
  The shadow has a very subtle neon colour fringe where it meets the light source.
GLOW EFFECTS: Products and key typographic elements emit a soft outer glow
  (blur radius 15–30px) in their primary neon colour — this is the signature effect.
BACKGROUND ATMOSPHERE: Deep space darkness with subtle volumetric haze in the far field,
  suggesting atmospheric depth without a tangible environment.
COLOUR TEMPERATURE: Cool/neutral (6500–8000K — daylight to overcast sky range).
  All light sources trend cool-to-blue.
FORBIDDEN LIGHTING:
  - No warm tungsten or candlelight lighting
  - No soft, diffuse, haze-free studio lighting
  - No flat even illumination without neon accent sources
  - No hard white specular flares without colour tinting

==============================================================================
TYPOGRAPHIC_PERSONALITY
==============================================================================
PRIMARY TYPEFACE CHARACTER: Geometric sans-serif or technical/industrial sans.
  Think Eurostile Extended, Rajdhani, Orbitron, or equivalent space-age geometric.
  - Weight: Regular to SemiBold — heavier weights used only for single-word impact headlines
  - Case: ALL CAPS strongly preferred for headlines and subheads
  - Tracking: Tight to normal (0 to 0.05em); never wide-tracked for primary text
  - Character: Right-angle terminals, wide proportions, efficient legibility at distance

SECONDARY TYPEFACE CHARACTER: Monospace or technical grid typeface — for data labels,
  coordinates, version numbers, technical specifications.
  - Used for UI-layer text elements (dates, stats, system labels)
  - Colour: Electric Cyan or muted #6A7A9B

ACCENT TYPOGRAPHY TRICKS (Futuristic-specific):
  - Single letter or word with a neon glow treatment
  - Text with a horizontal scan-line texture (fine horizontal lines at 20% opacity)
  - Stencil cutout text revealing a gradient behind
  - "Glitch" effect on secondary text: offset duplicate in a contrasting neon colour
    shifted 2–4px from the original (used very sparingly, one element maximum)

TYPOGRAPHIC RULES:
  - All text must be legible — glow effects cannot reduce readability
  - No serif typefaces (unless brand identity requires it)
  - No handwriting or script fonts
  - No warm-coloured text

==============================================================================
SPATIAL_ATMOSPHERE
==============================================================================
COMPOSITION FEEL: Infinite depth. The design should feel like a portal to a larger
  space — a window into a technological world that extends beyond the canvas edges.
DEPTH OF FIELD: Product is in sharp focus; background elements exist at multiple
  depth planes with the farthest at 30–50% blur.
GRID SYSTEM: An underlying perspective grid reinforces the sense of dimensional space.
SCALE: Hero products feel monumental — scale them larger than real-world intuition
  suggests. This creates the "product as architecture" quality of Futuristic design.
ASYMMETRY: Strong diagonal compositions preferred over centred/symmetrical ones.
  Energy should flow from lower-left to upper-right (or the reverse).

==============================================================================
DECORATIVE_LANGUAGE
==============================================================================
PERMITTED MOTIFS:
  - Hexagonal grid pattern (honeycomb mesh) at very low opacity as background texture
  - Circuit board trace patterns
  - Concentric ring targets or reticle crosshair overlays (subtle, at 10–20% opacity)
  - Thin neon line borders or frame elements at canvas edges
  - "Energy ring" — concentric circular neon lines radiating from behind the product
  - Small UI data readout panels (displaying fictional specs, coordinates, or system labels)
  - Particle dot fields scattered across the background at low opacity

SIGNATURE MOVE: The "neon edge explosion" — a starburst of fine neon lines
  radiating from the hero product's centre point in both primary accent colours.

FORBIDDEN MOTIFS:
  - Organic natural forms (flowers, leaves, wood, stone)
  - Hand-drawn or sketch aesthetics
  - Gold or warm metallic finishes
  - Memphis-style solid geometric shapes
  - Luxury restraint aesthetic cues
  - Any element suggesting softness, warmth, or nature

==============================================================================
EMOTIONAL_SIGNATURE
==============================================================================
TARGET EMOTION: Awe and anticipation. The viewer should feel they are encountering
  something that exists slightly beyond the current moment — powerful, precise, inevitable.
BRAND ARCHETYPES: The Magician, The Hero — transformation through technology.
PACING: Dynamic but controlled. The eye is guided by the light sources.
ANTI-EMOTION: Never warm, never nostalgic, never casual or approachable.

==============================================================================
HARD_BANS
==============================================================================
The following elements must NEVER appear in any Futuristic-style generation.
These feed into the image generation API as mandatory negative_prompt parameters.

- warm colour temperature lighting, candlelight, golden hour, warm tungsten glow
- white or light coloured backgrounds, cream backgrounds, beige backgrounds
- earth tones, muted desaturated palettes, pastel colours
- organic natural elements (flowers, leaves, wood grain, stone, grass, sky)
- soft diffuse lighting without neon accents
- serif typefaces (unless explicit brand requirement)
- handwriting or script fonts
- Memphis-style geometric pattern fills
- luxury minimalism aesthetic (extreme whitespace, hairline rules, silence)
- warm metallic gold or brass surfaces
- photorealistic outdoor environments (parks, streets, nature)
- vintage or retro aesthetic cues
- watermarks, photo credit text, copyright overlays
- jpeg compression artifacts, pixelation, low resolution noise
- overexposed areas, blown highlights
- distorted anatomy, extra limbs, malformed hands
- duplicate product instances
- blurry text or illegible typography
