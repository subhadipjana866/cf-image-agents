STYLE_NAME: Luxury
STYLE_DESCRIPTION: Ultra-premium editorial aesthetic. Restraint is the loudest statement.
Luxury design communicates exclusivity through what is absent — generous negative space,
muted metallic accents, and an almost architectural sense of silence and control.

==============================================================================
COLOR_SYSTEM
==============================================================================
PHILOSOPHY: Tonal restraint with a single precious accent. Never more than 4 colours
in a single composition. Backgrounds are near-neutral; the hero colour is subtle, not bold.

PRIMARY PALETTE:
  - Deep Ivory / Warm Off-White:  #F5F0E8  (primary background for light variant)
  - Obsidian Black:               #0A0A08  (primary background for dark variant)
  - Champagne Gold (primary accent): #C9A84C
  - Pale Gold Highlight:          #E8D5A0
  - Warm Ash / Greige:            #B8AFA4  (secondary text, dividers)

EXTENDED PALETTE (use sparingly, one per composition maximum):
  - Antique Rose:     #C4938A  (warmth accent for lifestyle/beauty)
  - Slate Blue-Grey:  #8A97A4  (cool alt accent for tech/watch/fragrance)
  - Ivory Cream:      #EDE5D8  (background variant, product-forward layouts)

TYPOGRAPHY COLOURS:
  - Headline: #0A0A08 (dark) or #F5F0E8 (light) — pure, no tinting
  - Body/subtext: #7A7169 (dark bg) or #5C544C (light bg)
  - Accent label: #C9A84C — used for one element only (price, date, tagline)

FORBIDDEN COLOUR BEHAVIOURS:
  - No bright primaries (no pure red, blue, green, yellow)
  - No high-saturation gradients
  - No neon, fluorescent, or digital-glow colours
  - No colour gradients on typography

==============================================================================
MATERIAL_VOCABULARY
==============================================================================
SURFACES:
  - Matte white marble with faint grey veining — for pedestals, backgrounds, surfaces
  - Brushed gold / satin brass — for trim accents, ornamental lines, borders
  - Velvet — deep, slightly sheen, used as fabric backgrounds or product bases
  - Polished obsidian — reflective black for dark variant product surfaces
  - Uncoated paper / cotton paper texture — subtle grain overlay on backgrounds
  - Fine linen weave — for soft-goods or lifestyle product backgrounds

PRODUCT TREATMENT:
  - Products are rendered with a near-photographic precision, isolated with a subtle
    gradient shadow beneath them (not a hard drop shadow)
  - Floating mid-air or resting on a material surface — never on plain white unless
    the brand is minimalism-first
  - Reflections are soft and diffuse, not mirror-sharp unless the product is glass

==============================================================================
LIGHTING_ENVIRONMENT
==============================================================================
PRIMARY LIGHT SOURCE: Large softbox above-left, diffused through silk — produces
  even, shadow-free illumination on the hero product with no hotspots.
FILL LIGHT: Subtle warm reflector from right — fills shadows to a 3:1 ratio maximum,
  never eliminating them entirely.
ACCENT / RIM LIGHT: Single thin champagne-gold rim light from upper-right — defines
  the product edge without flaring.
SHADOW STYLE: Soft, long shadow falling towards lower-right at 15–20° from vertical.
  Shadows are warm (not cold/blue). Maximum shadow density at 30% opacity.
BACKGROUND LIGHT: Gradual vignette towards edges, centre is the brightest point.
COLOUR TEMPERATURE: 3200–3800K (warm candlelight to studio tungsten range). Never cool.
FORBIDDEN LIGHTING:
  - No hard shadows with sharp edges
  - No dramatic spotlight pools
  - No neon or coloured gel lighting
  - No blown highlights or overexposed areas

==============================================================================
TYPOGRAPHIC_PERSONALITY
==============================================================================
PRIMARY TYPEFACE CHARACTER: High-contrast serif — tall x-height, razor-thin hairlines,
  generous optical spacing. Think Didot, Bodoni, or editorial fashion mastheads.
  - Weight: Light to Regular for headlines; never bold
  - Tracking: Expanded (0.08–0.15em) for luxury breathing room
  - Leading: 1.1–1.2 × font size for ultra-tight editorial stacking

SECONDARY TYPEFACE CHARACTER: Minimal sans-serif — geometric or humanist, ultra-light
  weight only. Used for subtext, labels, dates.
  - Weight: ExtraLight or Thin
  - Tracking: Wide (0.15–0.25em for all-caps labels)
  - Case: Sentence case for body; ALL CAPS with expanded tracking for labels only

ACCENT TYPOGRAPHY: Single word or phrase in #C9A84C — the most precious typographic
  element. Used for taglines, edition labels, or price-point calls.

TYPOGRAPHIC RULES:
  - Maximum 2 typeface families per composition
  - No drop shadows on text
  - No outlined/hollow text
  - No decorative scripts unless brand identity requires it
  - Text never overlaps the hero product's core body

==============================================================================
SPATIAL_ATMOSPHERE
==============================================================================
COMPOSITION FEEL: Architectural stillness. The image should feel like a museum vitrine.
  Negative space is intentional — at least 35% of the canvas is empty or near-empty.
DEPTH OF FIELD: Shallow focus on the hero product; background subtly soft but never
  bokeh-heavy. The soft-focus is editorial, not cinematic.
BACKGROUND DEPTH: Subtle tonal gradient from centre-light to edge-dark creates depth
  without a tangible environment. Alternatively, a single flat surface at camera angle.
CROP & FRAMING: Generous breathing room around the hero. Never tight-cropped to edges.
  Products positioned in upper-centre or golden-ratio sweet spots.

==============================================================================
DECORATIVE_LANGUAGE
==============================================================================
PERMITTED MOTIFS:
  - Single thin gold hairline rule (0.5–1pt) as a horizontal or vertical divider
  - Monogram or brand mark at a scale no larger than 5% canvas height
  - Fine geometric frame (unfilled rectangle) set back from canvas edges by 3–4%
  - Minimal botanical silhouette as a secondary compositional element (never dominant)

FORBIDDEN MOTIFS:
  - Patterns, textures, or repeating geometric fills
  - Badge or starburst graphics
  - Ribbons, banners, flourishes
  - Any element that reads as "discount" or "sale" visual language
  - Drop shadows on decorative elements

==============================================================================
EMOTIONAL_SIGNATURE
==============================================================================
TARGET EMOTION: Reverence. The viewer should feel they are encountering something
  rare and considered. Desire is cultivated through restraint, not excitement.
BRAND ARCHETYPES: The Sage, The Ruler — authority through precision.
PACING: Slow. The design rewards careful looking.
ANTI-EMOTION: Never frantic, never playful, never loud.

==============================================================================
HARD_BANS
==============================================================================
The following elements must NEVER appear in any Luxury-style generation.
These feed into the image generation API as mandatory negative_prompt parameters.

- bright saturated colours, rainbow palettes, neon colours, fluorescent colours
- bold heavy typography, thick font weights
- drop shadows on text
- starburst graphics, badge graphics, ribbon graphics, banner graphics
- sale percentage callouts in large bold type
- cluttered busy compositions, multiple competing focal points
- cartoon or illustrative elements
- pixel art, retro 8-bit aesthetics
- stock photo generic backgrounds (city skylines, grass fields)
- watermarks, visible photo credit text
- overexposed areas, blown highlights, harsh specular flares
- jpeg compression artifacts, pixelation, low resolution noise
- extra limbs, distorted anatomy, malformed hands
- duplicate product instances
- text that is blurry, distorted, or difficult to read
- patterns that tile visibly across the background
