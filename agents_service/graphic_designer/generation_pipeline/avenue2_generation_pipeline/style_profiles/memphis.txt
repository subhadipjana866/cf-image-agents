STYLE_NAME: Memphis
STYLE_DESCRIPTION: The Memphis Design Group (1980s Milan) brought radical graphic joy —
geometric shapes in clashing primaries, irreverent patterns, and a deliberate rejection
of "good taste." Modern Memphis reinterprets this as controlled chaos: bold, energetic,
and culturally loaded with an optimistic maximalism.

==============================================================================
COLOR_SYSTEM
==============================================================================
PHILOSOPHY: Maximum chromatic energy through contrast and saturation. Memphis does not
neutralise — it collides. Primary triads are deployed unapologetically, with black and
white as structural anchors.

PRIMARY PALETTE (Core Memphis):
  - Shock Yellow:        #FFE03D  (primary background or dominant fill)
  - Hot Coral / Red:     #FF4B4B  (accent, shape fills, typographic pops)
  - Electric Blue:       #1E56FF  (counter-accent, backgrounds, geometric fills)
  - Stark White:         #FFFFFF  (negative space, shape infill, text ground)
  - Jet Black:           #0D0D0D  (structural lines, bold text, outlines)

SECONDARY PALETTE (Modern Memphis extensions):
  - Acid Green:          #7BFF4A  (third accent for triple-triad compositions)
  - Candy Pink:          #FF7ABB  (lifestyle or beauty-adjacent campaigns)
  - Teal-Turquoise:      #00C4B4  (tech or youth campaigns)
  - Deep Purple:         #6600CC  (premium-youth crossover compositions)

BACKGROUND VARIANTS:
  A. "Checkerboard Ground": alternating Shock Yellow / White tiles in a fine grid
  B. "Solid Burst": single flat primary colour with pattern overlay in contrasting colour
  C. "White Canvas": flat white background with Memphis shapes as foreground elements

FORBIDDEN COLOUR BEHAVIOURS:
  - No muted, desaturated, or earth tones
  - No gradients (unless a deliberate 2-colour hard gradient, not a soft blend)
  - No black backgrounds (Memphis lives in light and colour)
  - No single-colour monochromatic compositions

==============================================================================
MATERIAL_VOCABULARY
==============================================================================
SURFACES:
  - Flat colour fills — pure pigment, no texture, no shading within shapes
  - Pattern overlays: polka dots, diagonal stripes, squiggly lines, triangle clusters,
    checkerboard grids — rendered as flat graphic elements, not photographic textures
  - Laminate / glossy plastic — products rendered with a clean product-photo look
    against the graphic background, not blended into it
  - Hard-edge geometric shapes: circles, triangles, right-angle rectangles used
    as compositional elements and framing devices

PRODUCT TREATMENT:
  - Products are photographically realistic but placed as graphic-design objects —
    they sit on or within geometric shape arrangements, never blended into the background
  - Products may be outlined with a 3–4pt black stroke for graphic isolation
  - No product drop shadows — use flat geometric shadow shapes (parallelogram offset)
    in a contrasting solid colour instead

==============================================================================
LIGHTING_ENVIRONMENT
==============================================================================
PRIMARY LIGHT: Flat, even studio lighting — no visible directional shadow play
  on the background. The background reads as 100% graphic, not photographic.
PRODUCT LIGHTING: Clean, bright, near-overhead studio light producing even
  illumination. Products are sharp and clear — Memphis is not moody.
SHADOW STYLE: Flat offset shadow (a solid-colour geometric duplicate offset by
  6–10px in a contrasting solid colour) instead of soft drop shadows.
HIGHLIGHTS: Minimal specular highlights on the product only, used to confirm
  its three-dimensionality within the flat design system.
COLOUR TEMPERATURE: Cool-neutral (5500–6000K). Memphis is not warm.
FORBIDDEN LIGHTING:
  - No moody chiaroscuro
  - No warm golden-hour lighting
  - No bokeh or depth-of-field blur
  - No vignettes

==============================================================================
TYPOGRAPHIC_PERSONALITY
==============================================================================
PRIMARY TYPEFACE CHARACTER: Geometric sans-serif, bold weight — Futura Bold,
  Helvetica Neue Black, or equivalent. Clean, rational, unapologetically large.
  - Weight: Bold to ExtraBold
  - Case: Mix of ALL CAPS and Title Case (never all lowercase for hero text)
  - Tracking: Tight (0 to -0.02em) for impact; expanded (0.1em) for labels only

SECONDARY TYPEFACE CHARACTER: Contrasting slab serif or a second geometric sans
  at a different weight. Used for subheads or body copy.
  - Weight: Regular to Medium
  - Used as a visual counterpoint to the primary bold block

ACCENT TYPOGRAPHY TRICKS (Memphis-specific):
  - Text blocks rotated 90° on the vertical axis (for dates, edition labels)
  - Text on a coloured rectangular ground (text box filled with a contrasting flat colour)
  - Strikethrough, underline, or banded emphasis lines in contrasting colour
  - Stacked short words in very large type as a compositional element

TYPOGRAPHIC RULES:
  - Text is a graphic element — headlines can be part of the pattern composition
  - Black outlines on text are permitted (1–2pt stroke)
  - No script or cursive typefaces
  - No thin or light-weight typefaces for primary text
  - No drop shadows (use hard offset duplicates instead)

==============================================================================
SPATIAL_ATMOSPHERE
==============================================================================
COMPOSITION FEEL: Dynamic and intentionally busy — but structured chaos, not random.
  The grid underlies everything even if not visible. Shapes and text are on the grid.
DENSITY: Medium-high. Memphis fills its canvas. Negative space is used as a graphic
  element (a flat-colour zone), not as breathing room.
PATTERN DENSITY: Pattern fills cover 20–50% of the background canvas.
LAYERING: Shapes overlap and clip — Memphis does not fear collisions between elements.
SCALE CONTRAST: Extreme size differences between elements create visual energy.
  A large circle next to a tiny dot next to a giant headline.

==============================================================================
DECORATIVE_LANGUAGE
==============================================================================
PERMITTED MOTIFS:
  - Polka dot fields (varying sizes, single colour per field)
  - Diagonal stripe fills (2-colour, equal width, 45° angle)
  - Squiggle line clusters (free-form wave lines, 1–2pt, one colour)
  - Dot-dash border patterns
  - Stacked geometric shapes: overlapping circles, triangles, rectangles
  - Hard geometric drop shadow copies (solid parallelogram offset in contrasting colour)

SIGNATURE MOVE: The "shape explosion" — 4–8 geometric shapes (mix of sizes and forms)
  radiating from behind the hero product in contrasting colours.

FORBIDDEN MOTIFS:
  - Photographic or natural textures (marble, wood, fabric)
  - Organic curves (flowers, leaves, natural forms — these are anti-Memphis)
  - Gold or metallic finishes
  - Gradient fills within shapes
  - Any element that reads as luxury, minimalism, or restraint

==============================================================================
EMOTIONAL_SIGNATURE
==============================================================================
TARGET EMOTION: Euphoric optimism. The viewer should feel a shot of energy —
  joyful, stimulated, and slightly overwhelmed in the best possible way.
BRAND ARCHETYPES: The Jester, The Creator — irreverence through abundance.
PACING: Fast. The eye moves constantly.
ANTI-EMOTION: Never sombre, never quiet, never understated.

==============================================================================
HARD_BANS
==============================================================================
The following elements must NEVER appear in any Memphis-style generation.
These feed into the image generation API as mandatory negative_prompt parameters.

- muted tones, earth tones, desaturated palette, monochromatic grey compositions
- soft gradients, radial blending, photographic sky gradients
- black or very dark backgrounds
- thin lightweight typography, delicate serif fonts
- minimalist compositions with excessive white space
- marble texture, gold metallic surfaces, velvet textures
- moody atmospheric lighting, bokeh backgrounds, depth of field blur
- vignettes, dark corners
- organic natural forms (flowers, leaves, wood, stone)
- photorealistic environmental backgrounds (cityscapes, interiors, outdoors)
- soft drop shadows (use flat geometric offset shadows instead)
- watermarks, copyright text, photo credit overlays
- jpeg compression artifacts, pixelation, blurry text
- overexposed areas, blown highlights
- distorted anatomy, extra limbs, malformed hands
- duplicate product instances
- luxury minimalism aesthetic cues
